## Wiki Purpose

Per the provided wiki pattern, this wiki is the **persistent knowledge layer** for the repo:

- **Raw source**: the root `README.md` (scene text and notes).
- **Wiki**: structured, summarized pages in this `wiki/` directory.
- **Schema**: this process and these page conventions, documented in `schema.md`.

It keeps the high-level meaning of the story project discoverable without rereading the full scene text every time.
